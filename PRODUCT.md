# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and makers using a laptop beside ESP32/Pico boards, sensors and small actuators. The initial user is preparing an AI-first hackathon project and already has hardware and computer-vision experience.

## Product Purpose

A small desktop companion that reads sensors and controls connected devices through direct controls or a natural-language assistant. The application exposes each board's actual capabilities and connection status. A built-in simulator makes the starter useful before equipment is connected.

## Brand Personality

Practical, quiet and precise. The interface acts like a small lab instrument and stays out of the way of the user's main work.

## Design Principles

- Direct controls remain available independently of AI.
- Show device state and errors honestly, including simulation and disconnected states.
- Keep the collapsed companion small and the expanded panel focused.
- Prefer predictable behavior and low resource use to decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing dashboards, decorative metric cards, continuously animated mascots and visually hidden simulated behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible labels and focus, keyboard operation, sufficient contrast and reduced-motion support. Color is supplemented by text. The first interface uses Spanish copy.
